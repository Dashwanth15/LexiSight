# LexiSight Pilot Outreach Templates

Use these templates to reach out to medical clinics, hospitals, and DHH (Deaf and Hard of Hearing) educational institutions for Phase 2 Pilot testing.

---

## 1. Medical Clinic / Hospital Outreach

**Subject:** Zero-Cost Accessibility Solution for Deaf Patients at [Clinic/Hospital Name]

Hi [Name/Title],

I'm writing to you from the LexiSight project. We’ve developed a real-time, browser-based Indian Sign Language (ISL) translator aimed at breaking down communication barriers in healthcare settings.

Medical miscommunication for Deaf patients is a critical issue that can lead to misdiagnoses. LexiSight solves this by turning any standard tablet or laptop into a bidirectional translator:
- The patient signs in ISL → The tablet speaks the English translation aloud.
- The doctor speaks in English → A 3D avatar signs it back to the patient.

Because LexiSight runs entirely locally in the browser, it requires **zero internet connection** after the initial load, guarantees **100% patient privacy** (no video is ever saved or sent to a server), and is completely **free of cost**.

We are currently launching our Phase 2 pilot in [City/Region] and would love to provide LexiSight to [Clinic/Hospital Name] at no cost. The pilot requires no IT integration—just a browser.

Would you be open to a 10-minute demo call next week to see it in action?

## 2. Educational Institution Outreach

We are looking to partner with forward-thinking educational institutions like [School Name] for our initial pilot program. We believe LexiSight can be a powerful tool for:
- Facilitating communication between DHH students and hearing teachers/staff.

Our platform is 100% free, requires no installation, and prioritizes student privacy (no video is recorded or processed on external servers).

## Tips for Outreach
1. **Personalize**: Always replace bracketed text and mention something specific about the institution's commitment to accessibility if possible.
2. **Follow-up**: Send a polite follow-up email 3-4 days after the initial outreach.
3. **Bring a Device**: If you get a meeting, bring a tablet with the app already cached and running to demonstrate the "offline" capability instantly.
